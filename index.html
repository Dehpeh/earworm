      How to play

      1. You hear the first 0.1 seconds of a song.
      2. Guess the title, or skip. Either way the snippet grows: 0.1s → 0.5s → 1s → 2s → 4s → 8s → 16s.
      3. Seven tries. The faster you get it, the better the share grid looks.

      Snippets come from Apple Music's public 30-second previews, so the clip usually starts partway into the song rather than at the very first note.
